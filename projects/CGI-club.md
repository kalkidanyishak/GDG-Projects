# AASTU CGI Club — Website Requirements

**Club:** CGI Club 

**Version:** 1.0 

**Date:** March 2026

---

## What We're Building

A website for the AASTU CGI Club that does two things:

1. **A public-facing site** — shows who the club is and what they create
2. **A community hub** — a space for members to share work and get feedback

The design should feel creative and visually rich, since the club works in animation, 3D modeling, and graphics — the site should reflect that.

---

## Pages & Features

### Public Site

**Home**
- Eye-catching hero section with club name, tagline, and a call-to-action
- Animated or visually interesting background (particles, shader, or looping visual)
- Featured works preview (a few highlighted projects)
- Quick link to join the Telegram group

**About**
- What the club is and what it does (2D animation, 3D animation, graphics, modeling)
- Team/leadership cards with name, role, and photo

**Gallery / Showcase**
- Grid of member works — images and embedded YouTube videos
- Filter by category (2D, 3D, Graphics, Modeling)
- Click a work to see more details (description, tools used, author)

**Contact**
- Simple contact form
- Links to Telegram, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn

---

### Community Hub (Members Only)

Members sign up with their university email or an invite code.

**Feed**
- Members post their work — image, GIF, or a YouTube/Vimeo link
- Tag posts (WIP, Final, 2D, 3D, Tutorial, etc.)
- Like and comment on posts
- Mark a post as "Feedback Requested" to get more eyes on it

**Profiles**
- Each member has a profile showing their bio and all their posts

**Admin controls**
- Admins can approve/remove posts and manage content

---

### Courses & Resources

Keep it simple — no video hosting, just links and embeds.

- Cards linking to tutorials or resources (Blender Guru, YouTube, etc.)
- Embed an unlisted YouTube playlist from the club's channel
- Organized by level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced

---

### Telegram

- A visible "Join our Telegram" button/link on the site
- Optionally embed a Telegram channel widget showing recent posts

---

## Design Direction

- **Dark theme** with vibrant accent colors (purple, electric blue, or neon)
- Smooth animations and transitions — nothing janky
- Should feel premium and creative, not like a generic university page
- Fully responsive on mobile and desktop

---

## Pages Summary

| Page | Who sees it |
|------|-------------|
| Home | Everyone |
| About | Everyone |
| Gallery | Everyone |
| Contact | Everyone |
| Hub (feed, posts) | Members only |
| Courses | Members only |
| Admin panel | Admins only |

---

## Nice-to-Haves (Later)

- Telegram bot integration for cross-posting announcements
- Member progress tracking on courses
- Club activity timeline on the About page

---

## Notes & Constraints

- No custom video hosting — YouTube embeds only
- Hosting should be free/low-cost (Vercel, Render, etc.)
- Non-technical club admins should be able to manage content easily
- Keep it simple for v1 — features can be added over time
